## Program overview

### Structure
The BSc in Computer Science & AI requires 126 credit hours: core courses, one specialisation track, general education and free electives. Students normally take 15 to 16 credit hours per semester over four years. Core courses are listed by the year in which they are normally taken.

### Recommended core sequence
Year 1 core: CS101 Introduction to Programming, CS102 Discrete Mathematics, MATH101 Calculus I, ENG101 Technical Writing, PHYS101 Physics for Computing. Year 2 core: CS201 Data Structures, CS202 Object-Oriented Programming, CS203 Computer Organization, MATH201 Linear Algebra, STAT201 Probability and Statistics, CS210 Database Systems. Year 3 core: CS301 Algorithm Analysis, CS302 Operating Systems, CS310 Software Engineering, CS320 Computer Networks, AI301 Machine Learning Fundamentals. Year 4 core: CS410 Senior Project I and CS411 Senior Project II.

### General education
Every student must complete ENG101 Technical Writing and HUM201 Ethics and Society in Computing before graduation. HUM201 is normally taken in Year 2.

## Specialisation tracks

### Artificial Intelligence track
Students interested in machine learning, data science, deep learning, natural language processing and computer vision follow the AI track. Typical sequence: MATH201 and STAT201 in Year 2, AI301 Machine Learning Fundamentals in Year 3 together with AI310 Data Mining and AI320 Data Visualization, then AI401 Deep Learning, AI402 Natural Language Processing and AI403 Computer Vision in Year 4. Strong mathematics (linear algebra, probability) and Python programming are the foundation of this track.

### Software Engineering track
Students interested in web, mobile and user-facing software follow the Software Engineering track. Typical sequence: CS202 Object-Oriented Programming and CS220 Web Development in Year 2, then CS310 Software Engineering, CS330 Mobile App Development and CS350 Human-Computer Interaction in Year 3, finishing with the Senior Project.

### Systems, Cloud and Security track
Students interested in operating systems, networks, cloud, distributed systems, parallel computing and cybersecurity follow the Systems track. Typical sequence: CS203 Computer Organization in Year 2, then CS302 Operating Systems, CS320 Computer Networks, SEC301 Information Security Fundamentals and CS370 Parallel and GPU Computing in Year 3, and CS401 Cloud Computing and Distributed Systems in Year 4.

## Advising notes

### Workload guidance
Courses are labelled Light, Medium or Heavy according to weekly effort. Students who want a lighter semester should combine at most one Heavy course with Medium and Light courses. Heavy courses are normally spread across semesters rather than combined.

### Capstone
Senior Project I (CS410) is offered in Fall only and Senior Project II (CS411) in Spring only. Students should plan the capstone for their final two semesters after finishing Software Engineering (CS310).
